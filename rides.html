Why “starting from”?

Delhi NCR is large — a pickup in Ballabgarh or Greater Noida can add 50 km or more compared to central Gurugram. We quote against your actual pickup point rather than charging everyone the same. The price you're quoted at booking is the price you pay for that trip.
